## 3. Recorder and replay

- **Which object poses are saved:** auto-discovered (`L/recording.py:104-114`): every body whose first joint is free, in body-id order. Welds = all equalities.
- **trajectory.h5 (L, format_version 2):** `t_wall`, `t_sim`, `rail_mm`, `joints_deg (N,6)`, `ee_pos_mm`, `ee_rpy_deg`, `ctrl (N,7)`,
  `body_poses (N,K,7)` (m + wxyz), `weld_active (N,neq)`, `gripper (N)`, each with a `units` attr; root attrs `format_version`, `state_hz`,
  `n_samples`, `joint_names`, `actuator_names`, `body_names`, `eq_names`; optional `/frames/<cam>/images`.
- **metadata.json:** session_id, started/ended_at_iso, duration_s, interface, task_label, outcome, demonstrator_id, notes, scene_xml,
  state_hz, n_commands, n_state_samples, kept, llm_model, llm_prompt, has_llm_log, parent_session_id, cycle_index, augmentation_config.
- **Replay (L and B):** always loads its constant scene (ignores `metadata.scene_xml`), replays `rail_mm` + `joints_deg` as ctrl at `t_wall`
  timing. **Objects and welds are not restored** — nothing is ever grasped in a replay. `replay_augment.py:423` (B) and
  `scripts/frame_from_summary.py:35` (L) are the only consumers of `scene_xml`.
- **B recordings:** v1 subset (no body_poses/welds/gripper/units). B replay reads only shared datasets.

## 5. MuJoCo

mujoco 3.8.1, Python 3.11.15, x86_64 (this machine). `MjSpec`, `MjSpec.from_file`, `MjSpec.attach(child, prefix=, frame=)` and
`frame.attach_body` are available. Measured: attaching the full lab scene under `arm0/` compiles with all 45 bodies, 12 actuators,
19 welds renamed (`arm0/act_rail`, `arm0/grip_translucent_cup`); two copies side by side compile (89 bodies, 24 actuators).
Not yet tested: a weld created in the parent spec between bodies from two different attached specs (robot gripper ↔ object).
