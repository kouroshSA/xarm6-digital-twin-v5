# basic_cubes_bins — the manual-control project's benchmark scene

The original cubes-and-bins scene of `xarm6_rail_sim_interactive_basic_v5`: a
primitive xArm6 on a 700 mm rail with three cubes and three matching bins, driven
in joint space (no IK, no gripper). A **passthrough** to that project's
`envs/basic_scene.xml`. It is the default room of `realtime_keyboard.py`,
`control_panel.py` and `terminal_control.py`, so they behave as before without
`--room`.
